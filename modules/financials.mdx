The **Financials** module (财务详情) provides multi-dimensional revenue reporting, giving you a complete picture of commercial performance across every layer of your operation. Rather than presenting a single aggregated view, Shejiu organises financial data into four distinct perspectives — Company, Influencer, Selector, and a Financial Details overview — so that finance teams, operations managers, and individual creators can each access the slice of data most relevant to their role.

## Financial Views

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Company">
    The **Company view** shows aggregate financial performance at the organisational level. Use it to track total GMV, blended order counts, and overall performance scores across all influencers and campaigns for a selected date range. This view is the starting point for executive-level reporting and period-over-period comparisons.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Influencer">
    The **Influencer view** (Fluencer) breaks down financial performance by individual creator. You can compare revenue contribution, order volume, and performance scores across your entire roster, or drill into a single influencer's trend using the date range selector. This view is essential for commission calculations, creator tier reviews, and identifying your top and bottom performers.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Selector">
    The **Selector view** attributes financial results to the product selectors responsible for sourcing items into campaigns. Use it to evaluate which selectors are driving the most revenue and orders, and to allocate resources or incentives accordingly.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Overview">
    The **Financial Details Overview** is a consolidated summary that combines signals from the Company, Influencer, and Selector views into a single dashboard. It surfaces the 7-day average metrics side-by-side with the selected period's actuals, making it easy to identify whether current performance is above or below your rolling baseline.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 7-Day Average Metrics

All financial average metrics across every view use a **7-day rolling window**. The three key averages are displayed consistently throughout the Financials module:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Average Revenue (7-day)" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    **7天均金额** — The rolling average of daily GMV over the past 7 days. Use this to assess revenue stability and spot upward or downward momentum before it appears in period totals.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Order Count (7-day)" icon="basket-shopping">
    **7天均单量** — The rolling average of daily completed orders over the past 7 days. Pair this with the revenue average to monitor average order value trends without building a custom calculation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Performance Score (7-day)" icon="star">
    **7天均战绩** — The rolling average of the creator performance rating over the past 7 days. This composite score reflects overall campaign effectiveness and is used in creator tier and incentive reviews.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The Financials module was previously configured to display 5-day averages. All four financial views (financialDetails, Company, Fluencer, Selector) were updated to the 7-day window simultaneously. If you reference historical reports or exports, factor in this change when comparing older figures against current averages.
</Note>

## Date Range Selection

All four financial views respond to a shared **date range selector** in the toolbar. To analyse a specific period:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the date picker">
    Click the date range field in the top toolbar. A calendar popover appears with start-date and end-date pickers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your start and end dates">
    Click the start date, then the end date. The selected range is highlighted in the calendar. You can also use the quick-select shortcuts (e.g. "Last 7 days", "This month", "Last month") below the calendar for common reporting periods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the range">
    Click **Confirm** to apply the date range. All four financial views update simultaneously to reflect the selected period, including recalculating the 7-day rolling averages relative to the period end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset if needed">
    Click **Reset** to return to the default date range (typically the current calendar month). This does not affect filters in other modules — the date range selector is local to the Financials module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exporting Data

Each financial view includes an **Export** button that generates a spreadsheet of the currently filtered and date-scoped data. Exports include all visible columns — including the 7-day average metrics — in CSV format, suitable for import into Excel or Google Sheets for further analysis.

When exporting large date ranges or high-volume creator rosters, allow a few seconds for the system to compile the dataset before the download begins. Extremely large exports (e.g. a full year across hundreds of influencers) may take longer due to the volume of data retrieved from the columnar storage layer.

<Warning>
  Shejiu's backend uses **ADB MySQL columnar storage**, which does not support CTEs (Common Table Expressions) or stored procedures. Custom SQL reports or integrations that rely on `WITH` clauses or `CALL` statements will fail against the Shejiu database. If you encounter query errors when building custom reports or connecting BI tools, contact your system administrator to have the queries rewritten in a columnar-compatible form.
</Warning>
