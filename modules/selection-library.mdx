The **Selection Library** (选品库) is a curated staging pool where products are assembled before being pushed into live influencer campaigns. Unlike the open-sea pool — which is broadly accessible — the Selection Library is a controlled workspace: products here have been evaluated for fit, assigned to specific campaigns or creators, and are subject to **time-limited availability windows**. Once a product's window closes, it is no longer actionable until re-added by an operator.

## Countdown Timers

Every product in the Selection Library carries an availability window defined by two timestamps:

| Field | Role |
|---|---|
| `lockTime` | The moment the product becomes locked / the window opens |
| `expireTime` / `expiretime` | The moment the product expires and is no longer actionable |

<Note>
  The backend API may return the expiry field in either camelCase (`expireTime`) or all-lowercase (`expiretime`) depending on the endpoint version. Shejiu's frontend handles both spellings transparently — you do not need to do anything differently, but be aware of this when building integrations or custom queries against the API directly.
</Note>

**How the countdown display works:**

- **Both `lockTime` and `expireTime` are present:** The system prioritises the end of the window and shows a live countdown to `expireTime`. This is the most common case — you see exactly how much time remains before the product expires.
- **Only one timestamp is available:** The system falls back to the single available value and counts down to that moment. The label adjusts accordingly to avoid displaying a misleading "time remaining" when the full context is unknown.

Use the countdown prominently displayed in each product card to prioritise your review queue. Products with less than 24 hours remaining should be actioned first.

## Brand Display

Brand names in the Selection Library follow a consistent display rule that accommodates products with Chinese-only, English-only, or bilingual branding:

| Scenario | Display format |
|---|---|
| Only the **Chinese** brand name has a value | Chinese name displayed alone (e.g. "耐克") |
| Only the **English** brand name has a value | English name displayed alone (e.g. "Nike") |
| **Both** names are populated | English first, `/` separator, then Chinese (e.g. "Nike / 耐克") |

This rule ensures that the brand column never shows empty cells or raw nulls, and that bilingual brands present a globally readable label by leading with the Latin-script name.

<Tip>
  When onboarding new brand partners, make sure both the Chinese and English name fields are populated in your product data to get the full bilingual display. A missing English name will fall back to Chinese-only, which may be less legible for international-facing campaigns.
</Tip>

## Reviewing Products

The Selection Library includes a structured **review flow** where designated approvers accept or reject products before they advance to the campaign stage. The review action bar sits at the bottom of each product's detail panel and uses a `space-between` flex layout — the **Approve** button is anchored to the right edge, and any supplementary actions (such as "Return for Revision") sit on the left.

**To approve or reject a product:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product detail panel">
    Click on a product row in the Selection Library list. The detail panel slides open, showing the full product brief, pricing information, brand details, and the active countdown timer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the product information">
    Check that the product meets your campaign criteria: correct category, brand compliance, pricing within acceptable range, and no expired availability window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the product">
    Click the **Approve (通过)** button in the bottom-right corner of the action bar. The product status updates to "Approved" and it becomes eligible for assignment to influencer campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reject or return the product (if needed)">
    If the product does not meet criteria, use the reject or "Return for Revision" action on the left side of the action bar. Add a rejection reason in the text field that appears — this note is visible to the submitter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Once you approve a product, the action cannot be undone through the UI. If an approved product needs to be withdrawn from campaigns, contact your system administrator to update the status at the database level.
</Note>

## Filtering and Search

The Selection Library toolbar provides several filters to help you focus your review queue:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Platform Filter" icon="filter">
    Filter products by their target distribution platform (e.g. Douyin, Kuaishou, Taobao Live). Useful when you manage campaigns across multiple platforms with different approval criteria.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brand Filter" icon="building">
    Narrow the list to products from a specific brand partner. Supports both Chinese and English brand name search — the filter matches against whichever name fields are populated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Status Filter" icon="circle-check">
    Filter by review status: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Expired**. The "Expired" filter surfaces products whose availability window has closed without a review decision — useful for backlog audits.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can combine multiple filters simultaneously. The active filter state persists within your session, so navigating away and returning keeps your current filter configuration intact. Clear all filters using the **Reset** button in the toolbar to return to the full unfiltered list.
