This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a blank browser tab to confidently working inside Shejiu. By the end, you'll have logged into the platform, oriented yourself within the top-level module layout, browsed the Open-Sea Product catalogue using filters, and pulled your first financial report. No prior experience with the system is required — just your login credentials from your administrator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the Platform">
    Open your preferred browser (Chrome or Edge recommended for full compatibility with Shejiu's Vue 2 frontend) and navigate to the Shejiu URL provided by your system administrator.

    You'll land on the login page. Enter your **username** and **password**, then click **Login**. If your organization uses a multi-factor step, complete that prompt before proceeding.

    Once authenticated, you'll be taken directly to the platform dashboard — your central launchpad for all modules. If you see a blank screen or a permissions error, contact your admin to confirm your role and module access rights have been configured correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Main Modules">
    Shejiu's top-level navigation is organized into five core modules, each accessible from the left sidebar or the main dashboard tiles:

    - **Open-Sea Products** — The full catalogue of publicly available products you can assign to influencer promotions. Products here are sourced from the open pool shared across the Baiying ecosystem.
    - **Selection Library** — Your workspace for assembling curated shortlists of products. Think of it as a staging area where you refine choices before pushing them into an active campaign.
    - **Operations Center** — Live dashboards and operational controls for monitoring influencer campaign health, tracking key performance indicators, and taking action on underperforming listings.
    - **Financials** — Detailed transaction records, settlement statements, and rolling average metrics. Use this module for reconciliation, reporting, and revenue analysis.
    - **Red Packets** — Tools for configuring red packet reward pools, defining distribution rules, and tracking how recipients claim and redeem their incentives.

    Take a moment to click through each section now. Familiarizing yourself with the top-level layout will make the rest of this guide much faster to follow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Open-Sea Products">
    Navigate to **Open-Sea Products** in the left sidebar. You'll see a paginated table listing all products currently available in the shared pool.

    To narrow the list to what you care about, use the filter bar at the top of the table:

    - **Platform filter** — Select one or more e-commerce platforms (e.g., Douyin, Taobao) to show only products sourced from those channels.
    - **Brand filter** — Type a brand name or select from the dropdown to isolate products from a specific supplier or label.
    - **Category filter** — Use the hierarchical category picker to drill into product verticals such as beauty, apparel, or home goods.

    Once you've applied filters, look for the **Priority Pin** toggle on any product row. Pinning a product moves it to the top of every filtered view for your session, making it easier to reference high-priority items while you work. Click the pin icon again to unpin.

    To view full product details — including pricing tiers, inventory status, and promotion eligibility — click anywhere on the product row to open the detail drawer on the right side of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Your First Report">
    Navigate to **Financials** in the left sidebar. The Financials module opens on the summary view, which displays aggregated revenue and settlement data for the current period.

    To generate a targeted report:

    1. Locate the **Date Range** picker in the top-right corner of the page. Click it and choose a custom range, or select the **Last 7 Days** preset for a quick view of recent activity.
    2. Click **Apply** to reload the table and charts with data scoped to your chosen window.
    3. Scroll down to the **7-Day Average Metrics** panel. Here you'll see rolling averages for key figures including gross merchandise value (GMV), order count, average order value, and settlement amounts. These averages smooth out daily spikes and give you a reliable baseline for performance comparisons.
    4. To export the current view, click the **Export** button (top-right, next to the date picker) and choose your preferred format. The downloaded file will reflect exactly the filters and date range currently active on screen.

    Bookmark this page or pin the Financials module to your dashboard for quick daily access during reconciliation periods.
  </Step>
</Steps>
